## Design guides

- [Prusa: Modeling with 3D printing in mind](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/modeling-with-3d-printing-in-mind_164135): overhangs, orientation and strength
- [Protolabs design tips](https://www.protolabs.com/en-gb/resources/design-tips/): a library of design-for-manufacturing articles

## Fastening and joining

- [CNC Kitchen: Tips and tricks for heat-set inserts](https://www.cnckitchen.com/blog/tips-and-tricks-for-heat-set-inserts): how to design for and install inserts
- [Spirol: Inserts for 3D printed assemblies](https://www.spirol.com/assets/files/inserts-for-3d-printed-assemblies-us.pdf): manufacturer's guide (PDF)
- [Sovol: Heat-set inserts and screw bosses](https://www.sovol3d.com/blogs/news/3d-printing-with-heat-set-inserts-design-strong-screw-bosses-that-last): designing bosses that last
- [Protolabs Network: Designing snap-fit joints](https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/how-design-snap-fit-joints-3d-printing/): cantilever snap-fit design

## Testing how things print

- [CNC Kitchen on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@CNCKitchen): strength tests of printed parts and materials
