# Assembling Printed Parts

[All resources](../README.md)

## Glue

- [3D Gloop!](https://www.3dgloop.com/): adhesives formulated for each printing plastic
- [JLC3DP: How to glue 3D prints](https://jlc3dp.com/blog/3d-print-glue-complete-guide): an overview of glues and surface prep
- [3DSourced: Best glue by plastic](https://www.3dsourced.com/rankings/best-glue-for-3d-prints-pla-abs-petg-tpu-nylon/): picks for PLA, ABS, PETG, TPU and nylon
- [Filament2Print: How to glue 3D printed pieces](https://filament2print.com/en/blog/kleben-teile-3d-druck): joint types compared
- [Sovol: Printing large models in multiple pieces](https://www.sovol3d.com/blogs/news/3d-printing-large-models-in-multiple-pieces-keys-glue-and-assembly-tips): keys, pins and relief channels
- [Adhesively bonded joints in printed parts](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40194-025-02048-9): a journal paper testing joint designs in PLA, ABS, PETG and ASA

## Screws

- [Hackaday: No need for inserts if you're prepared to use self-tappers](https://hackaday.com/2025/09/02/no-need-for-inserts-if-youre-prepared-to-use-self-tappers/): the case for self-tapping screws
- [Protolabs Network: Threads and screws in 3D printed parts](https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/how-assemble-3d-printed-parts-threaded-fasteners/): options compared
- [CNC Kitchen: Are our heat-set insert datasheets wrong?](https://cnckitchen.com/blog/are-our-heat-set-insert-datasheets-wrong): tests of insert hole sizes
