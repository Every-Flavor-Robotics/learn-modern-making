# Designing a Part

[All resources](../README.md)

## Drawing views

- [Multiview orthographic projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiview_orthographic_projection): front, top and side views
- [Isometric projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometric_projection): showing three faces at once
- [Engineering drawing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_drawing): an overview of the conventions, including section and detail views

## Designing around real parts

- [McMaster-Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/): hardware catalog with CAD models for many parts
- [Prusa: Modeling with 3D printing in mind](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/modeling-with-3d-printing-in-mind_164135): what to think about before you model
