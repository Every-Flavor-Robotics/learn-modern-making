# Onshape

[All resources](../README.md)

## Getting started

- [Education plans](https://www.onshape.com/en/education/plans): free accounts for students and educators
- [Onshape Learning Center](https://learn.onshape.com/): free self-paced courses
- [Onshape on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Onshape): tutorials and tips

## Going deeper

- [Versions and history](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Document/versions_and_history.htm): versions, branches and merges
- [FeatureScript](https://cad.onshape.com/FsDoc/): write your own CAD features

## Structuring your designs

- [Variable](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/variable.htm): named values in a Part Studio
- [Variable Studios](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/VariableStudio/variable_studio.htm): share values across tabs
- [Part Studios](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/PartStudio/part_studios.htm): how to organize parts, and when to split a studio up
- [Derived](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/derived.htm): bring a finished part into another Part Studio
- [Configurations](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/configurations.htm): several versions of one design

## Mixed modeling and meshes

- [Mixed modeling](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/PartStudio/mixed_modeling.htm): combining mesh and solid geometry
- [Working with imported CAD](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Document/working_with_imported_cad.htm): what you can do with imported meshes
- [MeshLab](https://www.meshlab.net/): clean up and simplify meshes
- [Polycam](https://poly.cam/): 3D scanning with your phone
